What we store

  • Stored: your e-mail address, the time you signed up, and which version of the privacy notice you agreed to. Nothing else.
  • Used and forgotten: your IP address, kept in memory for a few minutes to limit how many sign-ups one network can send. It is not written to the waitlist.
  • Not used: cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or any third-party script.
  • Where: the form goes to our backend at https://api.sloptopop.dev, which passes the address over HTTPS to a database on our own server. The database is not reachable from the internet.
  • What for: one e-mail when pricing is announced. Ask us and we delete your address; the privacy notice says how.

The sloptopop CLI and every language library are open source under MIT OR Apache-2.0, at github.com/sloptopop.
