FD&C Act section 524B (cyber devices) and FDA premarket cybersecurity guidance

21 U.S.C. 360n-2 (section 524B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, added by FDORA 2022) · in force; FDA final guidance of February 2026 (QMS considerations) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Sponsors of premarket submissions (510(k), PMA, De Novo, HDE, PDP) for cyber devices: devices that include software, can connect to the internet and could be vulnerable to cyber threats.

360n-2 Ensuring cybersecurity of devices
360n-2(a) In generalSubmitters of cyber device applications must include information FDA requires to show cybersecurity requirements are met. Coming soon
360n-2(b) Cybersecurity requirementsIntroduces the cybersecurity duties a sponsor of a cyber device submission must fulfil.
360n-2(b)(1) Sponsor must give FDA a plan for monitoring and fixing postmarket vulnerabilities, including coordinated disclosure. Coming soon
360n-2(b)(2) Sponsor must maintain secure-development processes and offer postmarket updates and patches for the device and related systems. We detect

arch.threat-model-missingFDA reads §524B(b)(2) as needing threat modelling across the device system; a networked unit has none.

secrets.committedFDA's guidance names hard-coded credentials as a weakness to avoid and test for; device software with them lacks reasonable assurance.

secrets.in-historyCredentials remain recoverable from the device software's history, against FDA's hard-coded-credential guidance.

secrets.insecure-defaultA device's software shipping a default signing secret is a default credential FDA's guidance says to test for; it lacks reasonable assurance.

tls.verification-disabledFDA's guidance expects external connections to be authenticated; disabled certificate checks defeat that.
360n-2(b)(2)(A) Known unacceptable vulnerabilities must be patched on a justified, regular schedule. Coming soon
360n-2(b)(2)(B) Critical vulnerabilities risking uncontrolled harm must be patched promptly outside the normal schedule. Coming soon

360n-2(b)(4) Sponsor must meet any further cybersecurity requirements FDA sets by regulation. Coming soon
