European Accessibility Act

Directive (EU) 2019/882 · in force since 2019-06-27; applies through national transposition · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Economic operators providing listed products and services (e-commerce, banking, e-books, transport, telecom) in the EU.
