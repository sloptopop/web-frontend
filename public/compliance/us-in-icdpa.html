Ind. Code art. 24-15 · in force since 2026-01-01 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Persons doing business in Indiana or targeting its residents that meet the Act's thresholds (e.g. personal data of at least 100,000 Indiana consumers in a year).
