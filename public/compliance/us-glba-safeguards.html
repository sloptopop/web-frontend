FTC Standards for Safeguarding Customer Information (GLBA Safeguards Rule)

16 CFR Part 314 · in force; last amended 2024-05-13 (breach notification) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Financial institutions under FTC jurisdiction (non-bank lenders, mortgage brokers, tax preparers, fintechs and similar) that hold customer information.
