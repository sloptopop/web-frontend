OSFI Guideline B-13 Technology and Cyber Risk Management

OSFI Guideline B-13 (July 2022) · current guideline dated 2022-07-31 (effective 2024-01-01 per mapping agent) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Federally regulated financial institutions in Canada (banks, foreign bank branches, insurers, trust and loan companies).

3.2 Defend
Principle 15 Design and maintain layered preventive cyber controls to safeguard technology assets. Coming soon
3.2.1 Secure-by-design practices are adoptedAdopt secure-by-design, preferring preventive controls applied end-to-end to applications, services and APIs. We detect

agent.as09.insecure-defaultInjection-prone query building, weak hashing or open CORS in in-house code shows baseline safeguards were omitted from an application or API.
3.2.2 Strong and secure cryptographic technologies are employedUse strong cryptography and protect encryption keys through their life cycle. We detect

secrets.committedA private key committed to a tracked file is exposed to every repository reader, directly showing key material lacks required protection from disclosure.

tls.verification-disabledTLS without certificate checks no longer authenticates the peer, so cryptography fails to protect authenticity and confidentiality.
3.2.3 Enhanced controls and functionality are applied to protect critical and external-facing technology assetsApply enhanced controls, hardening and extra defensive layers to critical and external-facing assets. Coming soon
3.2.4 Cyber security controls are layeredLayer security controls across the attack life cycle, segregating networks and minimising attack surface. Coming soon
3.2.5 Data protection and loss prevention security controls are implementedClassify data and protect it at rest, in transit and in use, including loss prevention. We detect

db.sensitive-column-plaintextA card, identifier or health column with no encryption or tokenisation lacks risk-based protection of data at rest.

tls.verification-disabledData in transit over unverified TLS is open to interception, so in-transit protection is missing.
3.2.6 Security vulnerabilities are remediatedPatch vulnerabilities promptly by risk, use compensating controls, and report on remediation status. Coming soon
3.2.7 Identity and access management controls are implementedImplement risk-based identity and access controls: MFA, least privilege, privileged access vaulting, monitored accounts. We detect

db.rls-missingWithout row-level security every role with table privileges reaches all rows, beyond least privilege.

secrets.committedCredentials committed to the repository are neither held in a secure vault nor securely managed service-account secrets.

secrets.in-historyA credential readable in git history is not held in a secure vault and stays exposed to everyone with a clone.
3.2.8 Security configuration baselines are enforced and deviations are managedEnforce approved security configuration baselines, disabled by default, and manage deviations. We detect

3.2.9 Application scanning and testing capabilities are employedUse static or dynamic scanning to assess changes for vulnerabilities before production release. Coming soon
3.2.10 Physical access controls and processes are appliedDefine and apply physical access controls protecting network infrastructure and assets from unauthorised access and hazards. Coming soon
