ETSI EN 303 645 Cyber Security for Consumer IoT

ETSI EN 303 645 V3.1.3 (2024-09) · V3.1.3 latest · voluntary · official text (free)

Applies to: Manufacturers of consumer IoT devices and their associated services that claim conformance (also used as a basis for national IoT security schemes).

  • 78 points
  • 0 we fix
  • 6 we detect
  • 72 coming soon
We fix
sloptopop finds evidence of a breach of this point in your code, and at least one of those checks has an automatic fix that has passed review on real code.
We detect
sloptopop finds evidence of a breach of this point in your code, and you fix it.
Coming soon
sloptopop does not check this point yet.

A check shows a breach in code; it does not certify that a point is met. List scraped from the publisher's document on 2026-11-01. A point here means: Clauses 5 and 6 and every provision, V3.1.3 (Void provisions and informative annexes, notes and examples left out).
