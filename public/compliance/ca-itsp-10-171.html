CCCS ITSP.10.171 Protecting specified information in non-Government of Canada systems

CCCS ITSP.10.171 (Canadian adaptation of NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3); basis of the CPCSC · effective 2025-04-02; second release 2025-10-28; assessment guide ITSP.10.171-01 effective 2026-04-20 · contractual · official text (free)

Applies to: Non-Government of Canada organisations handling GC 'specified information' under contracts; its controls are the basis of the Canadian Program for Cyber Security Certification (CPCSC) for defence suppliers (Level 1 launched April 2026).
