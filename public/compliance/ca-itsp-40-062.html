CCCS ITSP.40.062 Guidance on securely configuring network protocols

CCCS ITSP.40.062 (Practitioner series) · January 2025 edition · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Government of Canada systems protecting UNCLASSIFIED, PROTECTED A and PROTECTED B information (and suppliers bound by GC contracts); recommended practice for other Canadian organisations.
