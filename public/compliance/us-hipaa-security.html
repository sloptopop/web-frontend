HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR 164 Subpart C)

45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C (§§164.302–164.318) · in force; NPRM to strengthen the rule published 2025-01-06, not finalised (eCFR last amended 2024) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: HIPAA covered entities (health plans, clearinghouses, providers transacting electronically) and their business associates handling electronic protected health information.
