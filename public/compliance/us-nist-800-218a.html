NIST SP 800-218A SSDF Community Profile for Generative AI

NIST SP 800-218A, Table 1 (SSDF Community Profile for AI Model Development) · final · voluntary · official text (free)

Applies to: Producers of generative AI and dual-use foundation models, and acquirers who require the profile, for the software that develops and serves those models.
