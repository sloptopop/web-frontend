Texas Identity Theft Enforcement and Protection Act (business duty to protect sensitive personal information)

Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 521.052 · in force · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Every business (other than a financial institution under 15 U.S.C. 6809) that collects or maintains sensitive personal information.
