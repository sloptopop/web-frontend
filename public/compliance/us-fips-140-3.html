FIPS 140-3 Security Requirements for Cryptographic Modules

FIPS 140-3 · effective; published 2019-03-22, superseding FIPS 140-2 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Cryptographic modules used to protect sensitive federal information (and FedRAMP, CMMC systems).

A check shows a breach in code; it does not certify that a point is met. List scraped from the publisher's document on 2026-11-01. A point here means: The FIPS's own numbered points: the 14 announcement sections and the body sections. FIPS 140-3 adopts ISO/IEC 19790:2012 and 24759:2017 for its requirements; those are paid ISO standards and are not listed..
