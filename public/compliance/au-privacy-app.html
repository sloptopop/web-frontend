Privacy Act 1988 Australian Privacy Principles (APP 11) and Notifiable Data Breaches scheme

Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Schedule 1, APP 11 · in force; latest compilation No. 104 from 2026-06-04 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: APP entities: Australian Government agencies and private-sector organisations with annual turnover over AUD 3 million (and some smaller ones, e.g. health service providers).

A point here means: Compilation No. 104 (C2026C00227), the two parts this framework names: Schedule 1 (the 13 Australian Privacy Principles: principle, subclause, paragraph, sub-paragraph) and Part IIIC (notifiable data breaches: section, subsection, paragraph, sub-paragraph).
