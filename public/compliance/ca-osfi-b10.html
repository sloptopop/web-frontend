OSFI Guideline B-10 Third-Party Risk Management

B-10 · current guideline · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Federally regulated financial institutions and their third-party arrangements.

Principle 2 Establish a framework defining accountabilities, policies and processes to identify, manage, monitor and report third-party risk. Coming soon
1.2.1 The TPRMF is enterprise-wide and governs the lifecycle of third-party arrangementsFramework should be enterprise-wide, span the arrangement lifecycle, and be reviewed and improved regularly. Coming soon

2 Management of third-party riskManage third-party risk proportionately; higher-risk arrangements get more frequent, rigorous assessment and stronger controls.
2.1 Risk-based approach
2.1.1 Risk assessment criteria are comprehensive and scalableRisk assessment criteria should be comprehensive, periodically reviewed, and use criticality factors such as substitutability and exit impact. Coming soon
2.1.2 Level of risk of third-party arrangements are assessedDetermine risk level considering failure likelihood, financial health, subcontractors, concentration, and the provider's data and cyber practices. Coming soon
2.1.3 Rigor of risk management activities matches the level of risk and criticalityDepth and frequency of risk management activity should scale with the arrangement's risk and criticality. Coming soon
2.2 Risk identification and assessment
Principle 3 Assess arrangement risk before entering it and periodically afterwards, in proportion to criticality. Coming soon
2.2.1 Risk assessment
2.2.1.1 Risk and criticality of the arrangement are assessed throughout its lifecycleAssess each arrangement's risk and criticality before, during renewal, and upon material change, with a management plan. Coming soon
2.2.2 Due diligence
Principle 4 Perform due diligence before contracting and continuously, scaled to risk and criticality. Coming soon
2.2.2.1 A due diligence process is establishedSet up due diligence processes with documented escalation, approval and acceptance steps. Coming soon
2.2.2.2 Due diligence is performed proportionate to level of risk and criticalityDo due diligence scaled to risk, before entry, at renewal, periodically and on material change. Coming soon
2.2.2.3 Out-of-Canada arrangements are consideredFor providers or subcontractors abroad, review foreign legal requirements and political, economic and other risks. Coming soon
2.2.3 Concentration risk
2.2.3.1 Concentration risk is assessedAssess concentration risk by geography, supplier and subcontractor, before contracting and continuously. Coming soon
2.2.4 Subcontracting risk
Principle 5 The institution must identify, monitor and manage risks from its providers' subcontracting. Coming soon
2.2.4.1 Risks introduced by subcontracting practices are identified and understoodBefore engagement, understand the provider's subcontractors, its own risk programme, and concentration effects. Coming soon
2.2.4.2 Monitor and manage subcontracting risksEnsure ongoing reporting on subcontractor use, via contract terms such as notification, refusal and audit rights. Coming soon
2.3 Risk management and mitigation
2.3.1 Written agreements / contracting
Principle 6 Use written agreements stating each party's rights and responsibilities. Coming soon
2.3.1.1 Clear responsibilities are set out in the agreementArrangements need a written contract or agreement, reviewed by legal counsel, setting out each party's duties. Coming soon
2.3.1.2 The third party is expected to comply with FRFI’s provisionsStructure agreements so guideline expectations are met, including Annex 2 provisions for high-risk arrangements. Coming soon
2.3.2 Data security and controls (including data location)
Principle 7 Both parties must maintain measures protecting confidentiality, integrity and availability of records and data throughout the arrangement. Coming soon
2.3.2.1 Responsibilities for security of records and data are establishedAgreements should define data protection scope, access, responsibilities, liability, breach notification, and isolation of the institution's data. Coming soon
2.3.2.2 Record keeping requirementsStatutory records must be accurate daily, sufficiently detailed for regulators, and reproducible in readable electronic form. Coming soon
2.3.3 Information rights and audit
Principle 8 Arrangements should give timely, accurate information access and a right to commission independent audit. Coming soon
2.3.3.1 The third party provides the FRFI with information and reportingAgreement specifies the type and frequency of provider reporting, including performance and risk measures. Coming soon
2.3.3.2 The third party reports events that could materially impact the FRFIAgreement requires timely reporting of events that may materially affect risk or service delivery. Coming soon
2.3.3.3 Service performance and controls are evaluated, and audit rights established, as appropriateAgreement gives the institution and regulator rights to evaluate risk practices and access audit reports. Coming soon
2.3.4 Business continuity planning and testing
Principle 9 Agreements should cover continuity through disruption, and critical arrangements need contingency plans. Coming soon
2.3.4.1 Business continuity and recovery capabilities are established and testedProviders must have and test continuity and recovery plans; the institution's plans cover severe provider failure. Coming soon
2.3.5 Contingency and exit strategy / planning
2.3.5.1 Contingency and exit strategies are developed to ensure continuity of critical servicesMaintain documented contingency and exit plans, with triggers and playbooks, scaled to risk and criticality. Coming soon
2.4 Monitoring and reporting
Principle 10 Monitor arrangements to verify the provider keeps meeting obligations and managing risks. Coming soon
2.4.1 Oversight of third-party provider
2.4.1.1 The FRFI monitors its third-party arrangement(s)Monitor service delivery, provider financial soundness and emerging risks at individual and aggregate levels, proportionately. Coming soon
2.4.1.2 Metrics confirm residual risk remains within risk appetiteRegularly confirm residual risk stays within appetite, with metrics, thresholds and escalation triggers. Coming soon
2.4.2 Incident management and reporting
Principle 11 Both parties need documented processes to identify, investigate, escalate, track and remediate incidents. Coming soon
2.4.2.1 The third-party has clearly defined incident management processesEnsure providers have documented incident processes, including timely notification of the institution, covering subcontractor incidents. Coming soon
2.4.2.2 Incident reporting and notification requirements of the third party support FRFI compliance with OSFI’s incident reporting requirementsContracts must require prompt notice of technology and cyber incidents so regulatory reporting is possible. Coming soon
2.4.2.3 Internal incident management process is establishedMaintain internal processes for managing, escalating and tracking remediation of third-party incidents, with clear accountabilities. Coming soon
2.4.2.4 Incidents are investigated, analysed and results are sharedRequest provider root cause analysis for incidents, do own analysis as needed, and monitor remediation. Coming soon
3 Special arrangements
3.1 Standardized contractsDescribes standardized contracts: provider-dictated terms with little room for the institution to negotiate.
3.1.1 Risks of third parties with standardized contracts are managedRisk programme must cover standardized-contract providers, rate residual risk, formally accept it, and add resiliency measures. Coming soon
3.2 No written contract
3.2.1 Third parties with no written contracts still carry risksLack of a written contract does not remove risk; the programme must still address such relationships. Coming soon

3.3.1 External auditors comply with auditor independence standards when providing third-party servicesBefore buying consulting from the external auditor, confirm it meets auditor independence standards. Coming soon
3.3.2 The FRFI does not obtain actuarial or internal audit services from its external auditor unless certain conditions applyGenerally avoid obtaining actuarial or internal audit services from the external auditor. Coming soon

4.2 Third parties comply with the FRFI’s technology and cyber standardsEnsure higher-risk providers follow the institution's or industry standards, especially for access management and data protection. Coming soon
4.3 Cloud-specific requirements are establishedSet cloud-specific requirements, including data protection, key management and container management, with strong governance. Coming soon
4.4 Cloud portability is consideredConsider cloud portability and multi-cloud strategies to reduce provider concentration and exit difficulty. Coming soon
