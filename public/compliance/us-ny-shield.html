New York SHIELD Act data security protections (GBL 899-bb)

N.Y. General Business Law § 899-bb(2) · in force · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Any person or business that owns or licenses computerised data including private information of New York residents.

§ 899-bb(2) Reasonable security requirement.
§ 899-bb(2)(a) Holders of New York residents' private information must maintain reasonable safeguards, including data disposal. We detect

arch.privileged-key-client-sideA privileged key in browser code lets any user bypass access controls on private information.

db.rls-missingRows of private information are readable by any caller with table access, unlike the protected tables.

db.sensitive-column-plaintextHealth or identifier data stored unprotected can be read from any dump or backup.

secrets.committedA committed credential lets anyone with repository access reach systems holding private information.

secrets.in-historyA credential recoverable from git history still opens systems holding private information.

tls.verification-disabledAccepting any certificate exposes private information in transit to interception, failing reasonable safeguards.
§ 899-bb(2)(b) Compliance with the safeguards duty is deemed met via two routes.
§ 899-bb(2)(b)(i) Being a compliant regulated entity satisfies the reasonable safeguards duty. Coming soon
§ 899-bb(2)(b)(ii) Alternatively, a data security program with administrative, technical and physical safeguards satisfies the duty.
§ 899-bb(2)(b)(ii)(A) Program needs reasonable administrative safeguards, listed in the following points.

§ 899-bb(2)(b)(ii)(A)(3) Evaluate whether existing safeguards adequately control the identified risks. Coming soon

§ 899-bb(2)(b)(ii)(A)(6) Update the security program as business or circumstances change. Coming soon
