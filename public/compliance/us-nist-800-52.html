NIST SP 800-52 Rev. 2 TLS Guidelines

NIST SP 800-52 Rev. 2 · final August 2019; under NIST crypto publication review (planning note 2026-05-07) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: US federal agencies' TLS clients and servers (and contractors bound to federal TLS configuration).
