S.C. 2000, c. 5, Part 1 and Schedule 1 (CAN/CSA-Q830-96 principles) · in force; current to 2026-09-30, last amended 2025-03-04 · mandatory · official text (free)

Applies to: Private-sector organisations collecting, using or disclosing personal information in commercial activities in Canada (outside provinces with substantially similar laws for intra-provincial activity), and federal works and undertakings.
