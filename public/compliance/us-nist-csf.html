NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

NIST CSWP 29, The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 · CSF 2.0 published 2024-02-26 · voluntary · official text (free)

Applies to: Voluntary for any organisation; referenced by US sector regulators and federal agencies as the baseline for cybersecurity risk management.

  • 106 points
  • 0 we fix
  • 8 we detect
  • 98 coming soon
We fix
sloptopop finds evidence of a breach of this point in your code, and at least one of those checks has an automatic fix that has passed review on real code.
We detect
sloptopop finds evidence of a breach of this point in your code, and you fix it.
Coming soon
sloptopop does not check this point yet.

A check shows a breach in code; it does not certify that a point is met. List scraped from the publisher's document on 2026-11-01. A point here means: Function, category, subcategory of CSF 2.0. Implementation examples are informative and left out. Elements the export marks withdrawn (CSF 1.1 carry-overs) are left out and their numbers declared..
