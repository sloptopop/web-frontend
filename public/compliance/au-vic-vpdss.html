Victorian Protective Data Security Standards

VPDSS V2.0 · V2.0 current · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Victorian public sector organisations and their contracted service providers.

A point here means: VPDSS V2.0: the 12 standards (Standards and Objectives, 2019) and each numbered element under them (Implementation Guidance V2.4, 2026-01). Elements are OVIC's guidance on meeting a standard. Examples and primary-source columns are left out..
