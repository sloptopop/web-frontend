ANSSI SecNumCloud qualification framework

ANSSI, Prestataires de services d'informatique en nuage (SecNumCloud) - référentiel d'exigences · référentiel v3.2 in use; arrêté of 2026-08-12 (NOR PRMD2617964A) reportedly approves it under loi SREN Art. 31 (not verified on Légifrance) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Cloud providers serving French state administrations and operators that handle particularly sensitive data, and anyone requiring the qualification.
