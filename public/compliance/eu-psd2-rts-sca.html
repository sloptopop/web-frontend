RTS on strong customer authentication and common and secure communication

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/389 · applies since 2019-09-14 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Payment service providers under Directive (EU) 2015/2366 (PSD2).

Art. 1 Subject matterIntroduces security measures payment providers must implement under this regulation.
Art. 1(a) Providers must apply strong customer authentication as the payment directive requires. Coming soon
