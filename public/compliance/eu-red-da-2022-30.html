Compliance · EU

Radio Equipment Directive delegated act on cybersecurity

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (activating Art. 3(3)(d), (e), (f) of Directive 2014/53/EU) · applies since 2025-08-01; repealed by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/339 with effect from 2027-12-11 (CRA takes over) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Manufacturers of internet-connected, childcare, toy and wearable radio equipment placed on the EU market (from 1 August 2025 until 10 December 2027).
