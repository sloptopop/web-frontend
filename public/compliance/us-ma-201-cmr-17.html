Massachusetts Standards for the Protection of Personal Information

201 CMR 17.00 (under M.G.L. c. 93H) · in force (regulation dated 2009-11-13 on mass.gov) · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Every person or business that owns or licenses personal information about a Massachusetts resident.
