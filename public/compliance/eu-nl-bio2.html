Baseline Informatiebeveiliging Overheid 2

Baseline Informatiebeveiliging Overheid 2 (BIO2) v1.3, Deel 2 BIO-overheidsmaatregelen, on NEN-EN-ISO/IEC 27002:2022 · BIO2 v1.3 (2026-01-09) published in the Staatscourant 2026-03-05, replacing BIO v1.04 and BIO2 v1.2 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: All Dutch government layers and their suppliers by contract; also the NIS2 duty of care for the government sector.
