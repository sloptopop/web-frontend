Ontario Personal Health Information Protection Act

S.O. 2004, c. 3, Sched. A, s. 12(1) · in force · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Health information custodians in Ontario and their agents (and electronic service providers) handling personal health information.
