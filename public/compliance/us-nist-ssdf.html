NIST SP 800-218 Secure Software Development Framework

NIST SP 800-218 Version 1.1 · v1.1 final (Feb 2022); v1.2 (SP 800-218 Rev. 1) initial public draft of 2025-12-17, comments closed 2026-01-30 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Producers of software sold to or used by US federal agencies (secure software attestation under OMB M-22-18/M-23-16), and any organisation adopting it voluntarily.

We fix
sloptopop finds evidence of a breach of this point in your code, and at least one of those checks has an automatic fix that has passed review on real code.
