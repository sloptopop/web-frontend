Cyber Security (Security Standards for Smart Devices) Rules 2025

F2025L00276 (as made) · commenced 2026-03-04 after a 12-month transition · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Manufacturers and suppliers of consumer smart devices sold in Australia.
