OMB M-22-18/M-23-16 secure software attestation (CISA common form)

CISA Secure Software Development Attestation Form, OMB control 1670-0052; OMB M-22-18 and M-23-16 rescinded by M-26-05 · rescinded by OMB M-26-05 of 2026-01-23; the CISA attestation form remains available for optional use · voluntary · official text (free)

Applies to: Software producers that sign the attestation form for a US federal agency that still requests it under M-26-05.
