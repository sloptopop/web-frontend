Competition and Consumer (Consumer Data Right) Rules 2020, Schedule 2

F2020L00094, Schedule 2, Part 2, clause 2.2 (table of control requirements) · in force; compilation No. 10 from 2025-03-04 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Accredited data recipients (and CDR representatives through them) handling CDR data in the banking, energy and non-bank lending sectors.
