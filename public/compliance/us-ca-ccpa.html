Compliance · US

California Consumer Privacy Act and CCPA regulations

Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq.; Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, §7000 et seq. (Article 9: §§7120–7124) · statute and regulations as effective 2026-01-01; cybersecurity-audit, risk-assessment and ADMT regulations approved by OAL 2025-09-22 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: For-profit businesses meeting the CCPA thresholds that collect California residents' personal information; the §7120 audit duty applies to those whose processing presents significant risk (§7120(b)).
