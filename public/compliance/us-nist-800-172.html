NIST SP 800-172 Rev. 3 Enhanced Security Requirements for CUI

NIST SP 800-172 Rev. 3 (May 2026); SP 800-172 (Feb. 2021) as incorporated by reference in 32 CFR 170.2(a)(11) for CMMC Level 3 · Rev. 3 final May 2026, superseding SP 800-172 (2021, withdrawn 2026-05-13); Feb. 2021 edition still incorporated by reference for CMMC Level 3 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Nonfederal organisations handling CUI for critical programmes or high value assets when an agency contract selects the enhanced requirements, and CMMC Level 3 contractors.
