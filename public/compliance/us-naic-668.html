NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law (#668) as enacted by states

NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law (MO-668), Section 4D(2) · model adopted 2017; binding only as enacted per state · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Insurance licensees in states that enacted the model law (binding only through each state's enactment, whose section numbers may differ).
