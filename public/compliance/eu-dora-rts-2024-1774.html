DORA RTS on the ICT risk management framework

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1774 · in force since 2024-07-15; applies with DORA from 2025-01-17 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Financial entities under DORA's full ICT risk-management framework (Title II); the simplified regime of Title III is not mapped here.
