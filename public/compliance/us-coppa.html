Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule

16 CFR Part 312 · in force; amended 2025-06-23, compliance date 2026-04-22 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Operators of websites and online services directed to children under 13, or with actual knowledge that they collect personal information from such children.
