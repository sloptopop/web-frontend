PCI Secure Software Standard

v2.0 · v2.0 published January 2026; v1.2.1 transitions out 12 months after assessor training · contractual · official text (free)

Applies to: Payment software vendors listing validated software with PCI SSC.

The standard needs a licence click-through: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/ links PCI documents only through docs-prv.pcisecuritystandards.org, whose PDFs answered HTTP 403 with the licence-agreement page on 2026-11-01; the v2.0 announcement at https://blog.pcisecuritystandards.org/pci-ssc-releases-version-2.0-of-the-pci-secure-software-standard carries no objective list.

sloptopop does not write a framework's list from memory: a point appears here only when it comes out of the publisher's own document.

Reuse: PCI SSC licence agreement on download; cite control objective IDs only. Only identifiers, links and headings are shown, never the text; every summary and note is our own.

The sloptopop CLI and every language library are open source under MIT OR Apache-2.0, at github.com/sloptopop.
