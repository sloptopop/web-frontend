Queensland Information and cyber security policy (IS18)

IS18 Requirement 1 (ISMS based on the current version of ISO/IEC 27001) · v10.0.0, effective June 2026 · mandatory-for-scope · official text (free)

Applies to: Queensland Government departments and agencies (mandated QGEA policy).

A check shows a breach in code; it does not certify that a point is met. List scraped from the publisher's document on 2026-11-01. A point here means: IS18 v10.0.0: each of the policy's requirements and each numbered row of its reporting requirements table. Bullets stay in the requirement's text. IS18 holds no Annex A list: Requirement 1 adopts ISO/IEC 27001, a paid standard..
