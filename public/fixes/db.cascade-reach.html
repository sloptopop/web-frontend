Issue type · warning

db.cascade-reach

What it means. Deleting one row cascades into at least 10 other tables. It is reported at the table and at every JavaScript or TypeScript delete from it.

Why it matters. One delete silently removes a large part of the data.

What to do. Make deletes of business records explicit: use ON DELETE RESTRICT for records with their own lifetime, soft-delete or archive the root row, and delete dependent rows in code, inside one transaction, after checking they may go.

Automatic fix. None.
