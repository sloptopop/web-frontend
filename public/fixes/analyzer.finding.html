Issue type · error / warning

analyzer.finding

What it means. An analyzer reported a problem at SARIF level error or warning, and no accepted suppression covers it. The finding's origin names the tool and the tool's own rule id. Notes stay metadata.

Why it matters. It is the analyzer's finding. Its documentation says why it matters.

What to do. Fix what the analyzer reports, following its rule documentation.

Automatic fix. None yet.
