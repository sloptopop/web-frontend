Issue type · error

deps.unpinned

What it means. A dependency is declared without an exact version or commit, in a manifest anywhere in the tree: Cargo.toml, package.json, requirements*.txt, pyproject.toml, composer.json, build.gradle(.kts), *.csproj, Package.swift or CMakeLists.txt.

Why it matters. A new release, or a hijacked one, can enter the build without a change in the repository.

What to do. Pin an exact version or commit, and commit the lockfile.

Automatic fix. None yet.
